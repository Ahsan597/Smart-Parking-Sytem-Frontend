SMART PARKING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — FEATURE SUGGESTIONS FOR THE FUTURE
================================================================================
Last updated: 2026-08-16
New capabilities the platform doesn't have at all today — not fixes to
existing features (see IMPROVEMENT_SUGGESTIONS.txt for those). Grouped by who
it's mainly for, roughly ordered by how much value it'd likely add first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOR DRIVERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Ratings and reviews. Let a driver rate and leave a comment on a location
    after a completed booking, and show an average rating in search results
    — helps other drivers choose, and gives managers/admins real feedback.

  - A real payment gateway. Today, checkout just records which payment
    method you say you used (Cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Stripe) — no money
    actually moves. Genuinely connecting to one or more of these would let
    the platform actually collect payment.

  - A queue/waitlist for a full location. If everywhere is taken, let a
    driver join a waitlist and get notified the moment a spot opens — a
    formal version of what favoriting a location already does informally.

  - Recurring reservations for regular commuters — "reserve this same spot
    every weekday" instead of booking fresh each time.

  - Ability to extend a planned check-out time (or add one after the fact)
    without needing to check out and start over.

  - A quick way for someone without an account to book once — e.g. a QR
    code at the entrance for a one-time guest reservation, common in real
    parking setups where not every visitor wants to register.

  - SMS notifications as a backup to in-app/email ones, for drivers who
    aren't actively watching the app.

  - A native phone app (or at least real push notifications to a phone),
    rather than only a browser-based experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOR PARKING MANAGERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Bulk actions: mark a whole floor under maintenance in one step instead
    of slot by slot; import a location's whole floor/slot layout from a
    file instead of adding each one by hand.

  - Dynamic/surge pricing — automatically charge more during known busy
    hours. The peak-hours analytics already tell a manager when demand is
    highest; this would let pricing respond to it automatically instead of
    manually.

  - A dispute/support flow — let a manager (or a driver) flag a specific
    booking as having a problem (wrong charge, slot was already occupied,
    etc.) for admin review, instead of that happening outside the app
    entirely.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOR SUPER ADMIN / PLATFORM-WIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - An audit log — a record of who changed what and when (pricing changes,
    manager reassignments, deletions, role changes) for accountability,
    especially once more than one admin is using the platform.

  - Two-factor authentication for manager and admin accounts specifically,
    since they hold more sensitive access than a driver account.

  - Exportable reports — download the analytics numbers (revenue,
    occupancy, peak hours) as a spreadsheet/CSV for use outside the app.

  - Promo codes / discount codes that admin or a manager can create and a
    driver can apply at checkout.

  - A loyalty/rewards system — small discounts or perks for drivers after a
    number of completed bookings, to encourage repeat use.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LONGER-TERM / BIGGER IDEAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Automatic vehicle recognition at physical entry/exit gates (camera
    reads the plate, matches it to a reservation) — a much bigger,
    hardware-integration feature, but the natural next step if this is ever
    deployed at a real, physical, staffed-less location.

  - True advance booking with a real calendar of future time-slots per
    slot (multi-day ahead, not just the current same-visit window) — a
    bigger redesign than what exists today, mentioned here as the natural
    next step if same-day scheduling (already built) turns out not to be
    enough.
