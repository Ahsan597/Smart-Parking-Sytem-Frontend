================================================================================
SMART PARKING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — DETAILED PROJECT DOCUMENTATION
================================================================================
Last updated: 2026-08-16
Purpose: a complete, plain-language walkthrough of what this system does and
how it behaves — every feature, every role, every rule — written so it can be
understood on its own years from now, without needing to read any code.

================================================================================
1. WHAT THIS SYSTEM IS
================================================================================
A parking management platform that connects three kinds of people:

  - A DRIVER, who wants to find, reserve, and pay for a parking spot.
  - A PARKING MANAGER, who runs the day-to-day of one or more specific
    parking locations they've been put in charge of.
  - A SUPER ADMIN, who oversees the whole platform — every location, every
    manager, every driver, and platform-wide numbers.

Each of the three signs in and lands on their own dashboard, built around
what that role actually needs to do. A driver never sees admin tools; a
manager only ever sees their own assigned locations; the admin sees and can
reach into everything.

The physical world this models: a PARKING LOCATION (e.g. "Parking A" on Main
Blvd) is made up of one or more FLOORS, and each floor has individual
PARKING SLOTS. A slot has a type (Normal, VIP, EV charging, or Disabled
parking) and, at any moment, a status: Available, Reserved, Occupied, or
Under Maintenance.

================================================================================
2. ACCOUNTS
================================================================================

REGISTRATION AND LOGIN
Anyone can create their own account by registering with a full name, email,
password, and optional phone number — this always creates a DRIVER account.
Manager and Admin accounts are not self-registered; see their sections
below for how those get created. Logging in keeps you signed in across
browser refreshes until you log out.

PROFILE
Every signed-in person — driver, manager, or admin — has a "Profile" page
where they can update their full name, phone number, and a profile picture
(entered as an image link). Email cannot be changed here. Saving updates
take effect immediately everywhere your name is shown (e.g. the header).

ROLES
There are exactly three roles: Driver, Parking Manager, and Super Admin. A
person's role decides which part of the platform they land on after login
and what they're allowed to do. Today, a driver cannot be promoted to
manager through the interface — a manager account is created directly by an
admin as a manager from the start (see Section 4).

================================================================================
3. THE DRIVER EXPERIENCE
================================================================================

3.1 DASHBOARD
After logging in, a driver lands on their Dashboard: a summary card showing
who they're logged in as, and a list of their vehicles. Vehicles are added
through an "Add Vehicle" button that opens a small popup form — vehicle
number/plate, type (Car, Bike, SUV, Truck, Van, or EV), and optional brand
and model. A driver needs at least one vehicle on file before they can
reserve any parking slot.

3.2 FINDING PARKING
The "Find Parking" page lets a driver search parking locations by city
and/or name, with an optional filter to only show locations that currently
have at least one available slot. Each result shows the location's name,
address, hourly price (or "Pricing not set" if the manager/admin hasn't
configured pricing yet), and how many slots are available right now. Each
location can be starred as a favorite directly from this list (see 3.6).

3.3 VIEWING A LOCATION'S SLOTS
Opening a location shows its available slots grouped by floor, along with
the location's pricing. This view updates itself live — if another driver
reserves the last available slot while you're looking at the page, it
disappears from the list automatically, with no need to refresh.

3.4 RESERVING A SLOT
Clicking "Reserve" on a slot opens the reservation form:
  - Pick which of your vehicles this reservation is for.
  - Choose when you're checking in: either "Check in now" (the default —
    you're arriving immediately), or "Pick a time" to schedule a check-in
    up to 2 hours from now (useful if you're on your way but not there yet).
  - Optionally, set a planned check-out time if you already know when
    you'll be leaving. This is entirely optional — if you don't know yet,
    leave it blank. You are never charged based on this planned time; it's
    just for your own and the manager's reference. What you're actually
    charged is always based on the real time between when you check in and
    when you check out (see 3.7).

A vehicle can only have ONE active reservation at a time (either "Reserved"
or "Checked In") — trying to reserve a second slot with a vehicle that
already has one in progress is rejected with a clear message.

3.5 IF YOU DON'T CHECK IN IN TIME
A reservation automatically expires — and the slot becomes available for
someone else — if you don't check in within 15 minutes of your scheduled
check-in time. If you chose "Check in now," that's 15 minutes from the
moment you reserved. If you scheduled a check-in for later, the 15-minute
window starts at that scheduled time, not at the moment you made the
reservation — so booking now for a check-in two hours from now does not
expire in 15 minutes, it expires 15 minutes after that two-hour mark if you
still haven't checked in. Your booking list shows a live countdown for any
reservation that hasn't been checked into yet.

3.6 FAVORITES
Any location can be starred as a favorite from the search results or its
own page. The "Favorites" page lists everything you've starred, with quick
access to view its slots. Favoriting a location has one automatic benefit:
you get a notification the moment a slot frees up there — you don't have to
keep checking back yourself.

3.7 CHECKING IN, CHECKING OUT, AND BEING CHARGED
"My Bookings" lists every reservation you've ever made, filterable by
status, with only the actions that make sense for each one:
  - A RESERVED booking can be Checked In or Cancelled.
  - A CHECKED_IN booking can be Checked Out.
  - Anything else (Completed, Cancelled, Expired) is just a historical
    record with no actions.

Checking in marks the exact real time you arrived. Checking out marks the
exact real time you left, and this is the moment you're actually charged:
the system calculates how many hours you were parked, ALWAYS ROUNDING UP TO
THE NEXT FULL HOUR (so 1 hour and 5 minutes parked bills as 2 hours; the
minimum charge is always 1 hour even for a very short stay), multiplied by
that location's hourly rate. You choose a payment method at checkout (Cash,
JazzCash, Easypaisa, or Stripe) — no real payment processing happens, this
is a record-keeping choice, not a live charge. While checking out, the
screen shows a live-updating estimate of what you'll be charged based on
time elapsed so far, updating every second, so there are no surprises.

Checkout will refuse to complete if the location's manager/admin hasn't set
pricing yet — you'll see a clear error rather than being charged nothing or
an incorrect amount.

3.8 CANCELLING
A reservation can be cancelled any time before you check in. Cancelling
immediately frees the slot back up for other drivers. Once you've checked
in, cancelling is no longer available — checking out is the only path
forward from there.

3.9 BOOKING DETAILS
Clicking "View" on any booking opens a full detail popup: the location and
address, which floor and slot, your vehicle, current status, the scheduled
check-in time, the planned check-out time (or "Not set" if you didn't give
one), the actual check-in and check-out times once they've happened, and
payment details once a payment exists.

3.10 PAYMENT HISTORY
A dedicated "Payments" page lists every completed payment — amount, payment
method, date, and which location/slot it was for — as a running receipt
history.

3.11 NOTIFICATIONS
A bell icon (visible on every page, for every role) shows a badge with your
number of unread notifications. Opening it lists them all, newest first,
with the ability to mark one or all as read. New notifications appear
instantly the moment they happen — no refresh needed — and pop up as a
brief toast message too. A driver gets notified automatically when:
  - A reservation is confirmed.
  - A reservation is cancelled.
  - You're checked out (includes the amount charged).
  - A reservation expires because you didn't check in on time.
  - A slot frees up at one of your favorited locations.

================================================================================
4. THE PARKING MANAGER EXPERIENCE
================================================================================

A manager only ever sees the location(s) an admin has specifically assigned
to them — never anyone else's. Manager accounts are created by an admin
(Section 5), not self-registered.

4.1 MY LOCATIONS
The manager's landing page lists every location assigned to them. Opening
one takes them into that location's management view.

4.2 PRICING
Every location needs pricing configured before checkout can ever succeed
there. A manager sets an hourly rate (required) and, optionally, a daily
rate and a monthly rate, for each of their locations. If pricing hasn't
been set yet, this is called out clearly with a warning so it doesn't get
missed — checkout will fail for drivers at that location until it's done.

4.3 FLOORS AND SLOTS
A manager builds out the physical layout of their location: adding floors
(a name and a floor number), and within each floor, adding individual slots
(a slot code like "A1", and a type — Normal, VIP, EV, or Disabled). Floors
and slots can also be deleted (deleting a floor removes all of its slots
too).

4.4 MANAGING SLOT STATUS BY HAND
On a floor's slot list, a manager can directly change any slot's status
via a dropdown — Available, Reserved, Occupied, or Under Maintenance. This
is meant for situations outside the normal booking flow, most importantly
marking a slot as "Under Maintenance" when it's physically out of service.

Important behavior to understand: manually changing a slot's status this
way does NOT interact with any booking or create any charge. If a driver is
actually checked in and a manager manually flips that slot back to
"Available" instead of properly checking the driver out, the driver's
booking is left in an inconsistent state and no payment is recorded for
their stay. The correct way to end an active driver's stay is the "Currently
Parked" / force check-out tool described next — not the status dropdown.

4.5 SEEING WHO'S ACTUALLY PARKED, AND STEPPING IN
Every location's page has a "Bookings at this location" section, defaulting
to showing who's Currently Parked (checked in right now) but filterable to
see Reserved, Completed, Cancelled, or Expired bookings too. For each one,
the manager sees the driver's name, phone/email, vehicle, which slot, and
(for anyone currently parked) how long they've been there; for a
reservation, the scheduled check-in and planned check-out times are shown
too. This list also updates live as bookings change.

If a driver needs to be checked out on their behalf — phone died, they
forgot, there's a dispute — the manager can use "Force Check-Out" directly
from this list. It works exactly like the driver checking themselves out:
a confirmation step, a choice of payment method, and the same real-time-based
billing math, creating a proper payment record. This is the correct tool for
ending someone's stay, not the manual status dropdown.

4.6 ANALYTICS
A manager has an Analytics page scoped automatically to only their own
locations (with an optional date range and a way to narrow to just one of
their locations):
  - Revenue: total amount earned and number of payments.
  - Occupancy: for each location, a breakdown of how many slots are
    currently Available / Reserved / Occupied / Under Maintenance, plus an
    overall occupancy percentage.
  - Peak Hours: a chart of check-ins by hour of day (0–23), showing when
    demand is busiest.

================================================================================
5. THE SUPER ADMIN EXPERIENCE
================================================================================

The admin can do everything a manager can (see Section 4's tools, reachable
the same way once viewing a location's bookings — see 5.4), plus platform-
wide administration.

5.1 MANAGING PARKING MANAGERS
The admin creates every manager account directly, via an "Add Manager"
popup — full name, email, password, and optional phone. This is the only
way a manager account comes into existence; there's no self-registration or
promotion path today. The admin's Managers page lists everyone currently
holding that role.

5.2 MANAGING LOCATIONS
The admin adds every new parking location to the platform via an "Add
Location" popup — name, address, city, map coordinates, and optionally
assigning it to one of the existing managers right away (or leaving it
unassigned for now). Right after creating a location, the admin is dropped
straight into that location's edit view specifically so that setting its
pricing isn't a step that gets forgotten.

From the Locations list, each existing location can be:
  - Edited — name, address, city, its assigned manager (reassigned to a
    different manager, or unassigned), its status (Active/Inactive), and
    its pricing, all from one edit panel.
  - Deleted — permanently, with a confirmation step first.

Only locations currently set to Active appear in this list. If a location
is switched to Inactive, it currently disappears from the admin's own list
too, with no way to browse back to it and switch it active again through
the interface — see the separate improvement-suggestions document for this
known limitation.

5.3 PLATFORM-WIDE ANALYTICS
The admin's Analytics page mirrors the manager's (revenue, occupancy, peak
hours — Section 4.6), but defaults to combining every location on the
platform, with the option to pick any single location to drill into
instead — not just their own.

5.4 VIEWING BOOKINGS AND FORCE CHECK-OUT, PLATFORM-WIDE
From the Locations list, each location has a "Bookings" link taking the
admin into the exact same "who's currently parked here" view and
force-check-out tool a manager has for their own locations (Section 4.5) —
except the admin can open this for ANY location on the platform, not just
ones assigned to them.

5.5 WHAT THE ADMIN CANNOT DO YET
Two things a Super Admin would reasonably expect to do directly are not yet
built as their own screens: setting up a location's floors and slots (today
this can only be done through the manager tools, meaning the admin would
need to be that location's assigned manager to do it), and changing an
existing user's role in a general way (today, a manager account is always
created directly as a manager from the start — there's no "promote this
driver" or "demote this manager" screen). Both are called out in the
separate improvement-suggestions document.

6. HOW REAL-TIME UPDATES WORK, IN PLAIN TERMS
================================================================================
The moment you log in, the app opens a live connection to the server that
stays open in the background for as long as you're signed in, and closes
when you log out. Two things ride on this live connection:

  - NOTIFICATIONS arrive instantly, the moment something happens that
    concerns you (see the list in 3.11), rather than only showing up the
    next time you happen to reload a page.
  - SLOT STATUS updates live on any screen currently showing a specific
    location's slots — a driver browsing that location's available spots,
    or a manager managing that location's floor — so if a slot's status
    changes for any reason while you're looking at it, you see the change
    immediately without refreshing.

This live-updating behavior does NOT apply to: the available-slot count
shown on the search results list (that only refreshes when you search
again), or the analytics dashboards (those only refresh when opened).

7. GLOSSARY — WHAT EACH STATUS MEANS
================================================================================

BOOKING STATUS
  - Reserved       — a slot is held for you, but you haven't checked in yet.
  - Checked In      — you've arrived and are actively parked; the clock that
                      determines your charge is running.
  - Completed       — you've checked out; a payment has been recorded.
  - Cancelled       — the reservation was called off before check-in.
  - Expired         — you never checked in within the 15-minute window, so
                      the system cancelled it automatically and freed the
                      slot.

SLOT STATUS
  - Available       — free to be reserved right now.
  - Reserved        — held for a specific upcoming/current booking.
  - Occupied        — a vehicle is currently checked in there.
  - Under Maintenance — deliberately taken out of service by a manager/admin.

LOCATION STATUS
  - Active          — visible to drivers in search, can be booked.
  - Inactive        — hidden from driver search entirely.

PAYMENT METHOD (a record-keeping choice, not live processing)
  Cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or Stripe.
